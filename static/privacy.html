1. Collection of Information

MediSight v3 collects and processes Protected Health Information (PHI) solely for the purpose of facilitating clinical care, institutional operations, and authorized research. This includes demographic data, clinical encounters, diagnostic results, and treatment histories. Information is collected directly from institutional electronic health record (EHR) systems via secure HL7 FHIR APIs.

2. Use and Disclosure (HIPAA Compliance)

We adhere strictly to the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) Privacy Rule. PHI is utilized exclusively under the Minimum Necessary standard. Disclosures outside of Treatment, Payment, and Operations (TPO) require explicit patient authorization, except where mandated by law (e.g., public health reporting).

  • Internal audits and quality improvement initiatives.
  • Coordination of care across affiliated clinical networks.
  • De-identified data aggregation in accordance with 45 CFR § 164.514.

3. Data Security and Storage

All data is housed within SOC 2 Type II certified data centers within the continental United States. Storage volumes are encrypted at rest using AES-256 block-level encryption. Access to physical infrastructure is restricted via biometric controls and 24/7 onsite security personnel.
